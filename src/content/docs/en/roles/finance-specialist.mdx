# Finance Specialist

## Main responsibilities

- Review payout readiness and withdrawal handling.
- Review reward configurations for business correctness.
- Validate that Wheel of Fortune amounts and weights match approved intent.

## Step-by-step: review wheel economics

1. Open the Wheel of Fortune section.
2. Choose a theme with stable contrast for the session.
3. Check all slot amounts.
4. Check all weights.
5. Review the derived chance distribution.
6. Confirm the business reason in the audit note.
7. Approve or reject the requested change through the internal process.

## Theme guidance

- `Dark` is preferred for long policy review.
- `Midnight` is useful when payout and status badges need stronger emphasis.
- `Aurora` is acceptable for short review and coordination work.

## Step-by-step: review payout activity

1. Open the withdraw queue.
2. Apply the required date range.
3. Review status distribution and detailed requests.
4. Export the precise subset needed for finance review.
5. Escalate mismatches between business policy and queue state.

## Not for this role

- technical access changes
- undocumented emergency actions
